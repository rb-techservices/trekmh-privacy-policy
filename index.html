Privacy Policy

Trek Maharashtra respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our app operates and handles data.

Information Collection

Our app does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. It only requires internet access to display fort images and related information sourced from public web data.

Data Usage

All fort details, photos, and descriptions are gathered from publicly available sources on the internet. The app does not track user activity, location, or device information.

Third‑Party Content

Images and information displayed in the app are credited to their respective owners and sources. If you believe any content violates copyright, please contact us for removal.

Security

Since Trek Maharashtra does not collect user data, there is no risk of personal information exposure. Internet access is used solely to fetch and display fort‑related content.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
